Why Convert Video to MP3?

Converting a video to MP3 is useful when you only need the audio. An MP3 file can be considerably smaller than the original video, making it convenient for listening or storing audio without the video portion.

The best bitrate depends on what you need. Lower bitrates can reduce file size, while higher bitrates generally preserve more audio detail.
